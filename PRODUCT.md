# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thomas and optionally a household partner. Primary context: checking finances from home or on-the-go — logging a purchase after shopping, reviewing the month's budget before payday, reconciling accounts at the end of the week. Bilingual: Vietnamese or English, user-selected. Usage cadence: daily to weekly.

## Product Purpose

A private household expense tracker. Records income, expenses, and transfers across multiple accounts; monitors budgets by category; provides a monthly financial health summary. Success looks like: open the app, know within 10 seconds whether the household is on track this month.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, personal. Speaks like a trusted household register, not a startup. Numbers are the voice; the interface is the paper.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard** — bland KPI cards, blue sidebar, soulless table-heavy layout. Feature-first over clarity.
- **Bloated bank app** — corporate navy-and-gold, promotional noise, heavy hierarchy designed to sell rather than inform.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers lead.** Data is the decoration. Color, weight, and scale serve the number, never the other way around.
2. **Quiet by default.** Color signals meaning — income is green, expense is red, gold marks action — not mood or marketing.
3. **Personal, not productized.** A household tool, not a product. No promotional copy, no gamification, no growth language.
4. **Both screens are equal.** Mobile and desktop are first-class surfaces with equivalent feature access.
5. **Language follows the user.** Vietnamese and English are equal. No hardcoded strings, no layouts that assume label length.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large/bold text. Bilingual (vi/en). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Semantic HTML throughout.
